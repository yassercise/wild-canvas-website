Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Wild Canvas is a hand-painting studio in Bahrain. This page explains what information we collect when you visit our website or message us, and how we use it.

What we collect

Our website has no accounts, forms, analytics or advertising trackers. We only receive information when you choose to contact us on WhatsApp or Instagram, such as:

  • Your name and phone number or Instagram username
  • The images, ideas and details you send about your design
  • Your address, so our designer can collect and deliver your piece
  • Photos of your item, including photos of the finished piece

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, give you a quote, collect and paint your piece, and deliver it back to you. We may share photos of finished work on our social media, but only with your permission.

Who can see it

Your information stays with the Wild Canvas team and our designer. We do not sell it or share it with anyone for marketing. Messages sent through WhatsApp and Instagram are also handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies.

Fonts and third parties

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit, and Google may receive your IP address. The buttons on our site open WhatsApp and Instagram, which are separate services with their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and order details for as long as we need them to complete your order and handle any questions afterwards. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by messaging us on WhatsApp or Instagram.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
